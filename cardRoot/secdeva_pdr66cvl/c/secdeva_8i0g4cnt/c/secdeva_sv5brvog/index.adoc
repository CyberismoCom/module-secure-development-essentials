This page runs the acceptable risk level test fixture and compares the policy check
failures on the risks against the expected results. The expectations are in the report
query, keyed by the title of each test risk, so the fixture can be
deleted and created again without changes to the report.

== What the fixture tests

The risk register of a development project (`secdeva/cardTypes/riskRegister`) has the
field "Acceptable risk level". The risk register of this test project sets it to "low".
secdeva applies two checks with this level to the risks of the project:

* *Controls must be identified unless risk level is acceptable*: the initial risk is
  above the acceptable level, and no control or task is linked to the risk with
  "is mitigated by".
* *Risks can only be accepted if the risk level is acceptable*: the risk is "Closed as
  accepted", and the residual risk is above the acceptable level.

ARL1 to ARL5 are risks in the risk register of this project. The task "Mitigating task
for the acceptable risk level test" mitigates ARL2, ARL4, and ARL5. ARL6 is directly
under Test content, outside a development project, and is the negative case: no
acceptable risk level applies to it, so neither check fails.
