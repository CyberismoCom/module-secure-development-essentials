== Registers

Registers are structured collections where you record and track security-related information throughout the product development. Each register serves a specific purpose and is referenced by the phase controls that produce or consume its content.


=== Register descriptions

* *{{#xref}} "cardKey": "secdeva_tihfcm7g" {{/xref}}* — Protected assets such as data, devices, systems, and suppliers.
* *{{#xref}} "cardKey": "secdeva_ogyw3165" {{/xref}}* — Cybersecurity risks expressed as the likelihood and severity.
* *{{#xref}} "cardKey": "secdeva_1lsdhpkr" {{/xref}}* — Concrete security weaknesses to fix: vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, or control gaps.
* *{{#xref}} "cardKey": "secdeva_71w7mnkj" {{/xref}}* — The security requirements your product must meet, including scope, boundaries, and target security levels.
* *{{#xref}} "cardKey": "secdeva_pfi5v0ii" {{/xref}}* — Evaluations of third-party technologies for business fit, quality, maintainability, and security.
* *{{#xref}} "cardKey": "secdeva_92of3r9c" {{/xref}}* — External requirements from authorities, regulations, standards, or customers that the project must satisfy.
* *{{#xref}} "cardKey": "secdeva_o4z9qwbi" {{/xref}}* — Recurring review activities against project status. Use these for milestone and checkpoint reviews.
* *{{#xref}} "cardKey": "secdeva_c2mcsp7r" {{/xref}}* — Documented project decisions on architecture, design, operations, or significant changes.
* *{{#xref}} "cardKey": "secdeva_1o52h5xj" {{/xref}}* — The secure development control framework, organized by phase (Foundation, Design, Implementation, Verification, Operations).
